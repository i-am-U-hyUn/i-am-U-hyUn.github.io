## Overview

On June 25, 2026, the **European Commission** announced its **preliminary finding** that Amazon's **AWS** and Microsoft's **Azure** should be designated as **gatekeepers** under the **Digital Markets Act (DMA)**.

---

## What Is a DMA Gatekeeper?

The **Digital Markets Act (DMA)**, enacted in 2022, is EU legislation designed to prevent large online platforms from abusing their market dominance. Companies designated as gatekeepers must comply with a set of obligations aimed at ensuring fair competition.

Gatekeeper designation is primarily based on the following **quantitative thresholds**:

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Annual revenue | €7.5B+ in the EU, or market cap of €75B+ |
| Monthly active users | 45M+ in the EU |
| Business users | 10,000+ in the EU |

In this case, however, the European Commission concluded that AWS and Azure can be designated as gatekeepers **even without meeting these quantitative thresholds**.

---

## Key Arguments Behind the Preliminary Finding

### 1. Qualitative Gateway Role

The Commission determined that AWS and Azure serve as **critical gateways connecting businesses and end users within the EU**, regardless of whether they meet the quantitative benchmarks. The DMA contains provisions allowing gatekeeper designation based on qualitative criteria when a service plays a strategically important gateway role.

### 2. Overwhelming Market Dominance

The Commission's analysis found that both cloud services significantly outpace competitors across several dimensions:

- **Revenue scale**: Dominant market share in global and EU cloud markets
- **Operational capabilities**: Data center infrastructure, service portfolios, and partner ecosystems
- **Market share stability**: Maintaining a strong position even amid AI-driven cloud demand surges

### 3. AI-Driven Cloud Demand Concentration

The generative AI boom has triggered explosive growth in cloud demand, and AWS and Azure have further cemented their positions as essential providers of AI infrastructure — a factor the Commission cited as reinforcing their gateway status.

---

## What Happens Next

This announcement represents a **preliminary position, not a final decision**. Both Amazon and Microsoft retain the right to:

1. Review the investigation file
2. Submit written responses
3. Present their arguments in hearings

The European Commission will consider both companies' submissions before making a final designation decision. If ultimately designated as gatekeepers, AWS and Azure would be required to comply with DMA obligations, including ensuring interoperability, providing data portability, and refraining from self-preferencing.

---

## Significance and Outlook

This move signals the EU's intent to extend the DMA's reach into **cloud infrastructure** — a significant expansion of scope. To date, DMA gatekeeper designations have focused primarily on consumer-facing platforms such as app stores, search engines, and social media. Applying DMA obligations to **B2B cloud infrastructure services** marks a meaningful new frontier.

If the designation is confirmed, AWS and Azure may need to substantially alter how they operate in the EU market. The outcome could reshape competitive dynamics across the broader cloud industry, both within the EU and globally.
