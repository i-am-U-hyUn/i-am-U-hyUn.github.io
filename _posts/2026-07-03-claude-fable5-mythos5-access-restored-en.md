## Overview

Following the lifting of a U.S. government export control measure, Anthropic has officially restored global user access to **Claude Fable 5** and **Claude Mythos 5**, effective July 1, 2026.

Through July 7, paid-plan users will receive a benefit of up to 50% of their weekly usage limit, after which the service will transition to a standard usage-credit system. The same day also brought news that Claude Fable 5 access was restored on Amazon Bedrock.

---

## What Is Claude Fable 5?

Claude Fable 5 is Anthropic's most capable widely released model, purpose-built for the most demanding reasoning tasks and long-horizon agentic work.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Context window | Up to 1M tokens (the maximum is also the default) |
| Max output tokens | 128K |
| Thinking | Always on (adaptive thinking) — cannot be manually disabled |
| Visibility of reasoning | Raw chain of thought is never exposed (an optional summarized view is available) |
| Assistant prefill | Not supported |
| Data retention | Requires 30-day retention (not available under zero data retention) |
| Pricing | $10 input / $50 output per million tokens |

**Claude Mythos 5** offers the same capabilities, pricing, and API behavior as Fable 5, but is available exclusively through **Project Glasswing**, Anthropic's invitation-based program — succeeding the previously invitation-only Claude Mythos Preview.

---

## Background: Why This Happened

Fable 5 and Mythos 5's exceptional capabilities placed them under U.S. export control restrictions, which limited access for users in certain regions. With those restrictions now lifted, users in those regions regain access to Anthropic's most capable models — effectively expanding the company's global service footprint.

As AI model capability becomes increasingly tied to national security and technology-policy considerations, export control shifts like this one are worth watching as a recurring factor affecting service availability across the AI industry.

---

## Impact on Users

- **Paid-plan users**: Receive a temporary benefit of up to 50% additional weekly usage through July 7.
- **After July 7**: Service reverts to the standard usage-credit pricing model.
- **Amazon Bedrock users**: Access to Fable 5 on Bedrock was restored the same day, enabling complex coding and knowledge work without mediation. The Bedrock version is described as featuring advanced visual perception and proactive self-verification for handling complex tasks.

## Industry Implications

This restoration carries significance beyond a simple service resumption.

**① Resilience of the cloud partnership ecosystem.** Access being restored simultaneously across Anthropic's own platform and cloud partner channels like AWS Bedrock underscores how a multi-cloud distribution strategy helps Anthropic diversify geopolitical risk.

**② Industry sensitivity to regulatory risk.** This episode reaffirms that the most capable AI models are disproportionately exposed to geopolitical risks such as export controls. For AI companies operating globally, building resilience against this kind of regulatory volatility is becoming increasingly important.

**③ An incentive to win back lapsed users.** The temporary usage-limit boost can be read as a deliberate move to re-engage the user segment that had been cut off from access during the restriction period.
